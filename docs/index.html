UACJ Paducah Monitoring Systems

Plant monitoring dashboards

Each system below polls equipment on the plant floor directly, records what it sees, and publishes a live dashboard you can open from any browser on any device. Together they cover log delivery, the presses, the Granco saw, and the aging ovens.

Pick a system to open its dashboard.
